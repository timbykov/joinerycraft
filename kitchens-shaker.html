The look

What makes a shaker kitchen.

Shaker is the most popular door style we make, and for good reason. The simple frame-and-panel door looks at home in modern, Hamptons and heritage homes alike.

Shaker doors come painted in two-pack polyurethane, in any Dulux colour, or in decorative board for a harder-wearing, lower-cost finish. Pair them with stone tops, a subway tile splashback and black or brass handles.

Key features

  • Recessed-panel doors in polyurethane or board
  • Any colour, from white to sage, navy or charcoal
  • Stone benchtops in 20, 40 or 60 mm
  • Subway tile or stone splashback
  • Black, brass or brushed steel handles
  • Blum soft-close drawers and hinges
  • Works in kitchens, laundries and vanities

FAQ

Good questions.

Are shaker doors hard to clean?

Not really. The recessed panel collects a little more dust than a flat door, but a damp cloth is all it needs. Polyurethane and board finishes both wipe clean.

Polyurethane or board shaker doors?

Polyurethane gives you any Dulux colour and a smooth painted finish. Board shaker doors from Polytec are tougher against chips and cost less. We can show you both in the showroom.
