The look

What makes a timber-look kitchen.

Timber-look kitchens use decorative boards printed and textured to look like real oak, walnut or Australian timbers. They give you the warmth of wood but resist moisture, scratches and fading far better.

For a Scandinavian feel, keep it simple: flat timber-look doors, white stone tops, matte black handles and plenty of light. Timber also works as an accent on overheads, an island or open shelving.

Key features

  • Woodgrain boards with a real timber texture
  • Flat or fingerpull doors
  • White or concrete-look stone tops
  • Matte black or brushed steel handles
  • Open timber-look shelving
  • Matching timber-look wardrobes and laundries

FAQ

Good questions.

Is a timber-look board as good as real timber?

For kitchens it is often better. Board does not swell, warp or fade like solid timber, and it is easy to clean. Up close it has a real woodgrain texture.

Can I match my timber floors?

We bring board samples to your measure so you can hold them against your floor. Polytec and Laminex have dozens of oak, ash, gum and walnut tones.
