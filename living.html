FAQ

Good questions.

Can you hide cables and power for a TV unit?

Yes. We plan cable holes, hidden power and ventilation for consoles and media gear into the design, so the finished unit looks clean.

Can you build a study into a bedroom or nook?

Yes. We design desks, overhead shelving and drawers to fit the exact space, whether it's a spare room, a hallway nook or a corner of the living room.

Do you make bookcases and display shelving?

Yes. Built-in bookcases, display shelving with LED lighting, bar cabinets and window seats are all made to measure in our workshop.

Can I match it to my kitchen or wardrobes?

Yes. We can use the same boards, colours and handles across rooms so your joinery looks like it belongs together.
