The look

What makes a Hamptons kitchen.

A Hamptons kitchen borrows from American coastal homes: light, airy and a little bit grand. It works best in open-plan rooms where the kitchen is on show, and it suits both new builds and older Sydney homes.

The look comes from the details. Shaker doors with a simple recessed panel, crisp white or soft grey paint, a big island with seating, and brass or black handles. Decorative panels on the island ends and a rangehood surround finish it off.

Key features

  • Shaker doors in polyurethane or board
  • Soft whites, creams, sage or navy
  • Marble-look stone, often 40 mm thick
  • Island with seating and panelled ends
  • Brushed brass or black cup and knob handles
  • Open shelves or glass-front overheads
  • Rangehood surround and decorative mouldings

FAQ

Good questions.

Is a Hamptons kitchen expensive?

It can cost more than a plain flat-door kitchen because of shaker doors, mouldings and thicker stone. You can keep costs down with board shaker doors instead of polyurethane, and a 20 mm benchtop. We will price both options in your quote.

Do Hamptons kitchens suit small spaces?

Yes. Keep the colours light, use shaker doors on a galley or L-shaped layout, and skip the island if space is tight. You still get the look without crowding the room.
