Privacy

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

When you ask for a quote or contact us, we collect the details you choose to give us, such as your name, phone number, email address, suburb, project details and any photos or plans you upload.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare your design and quote
  • To book measures, showroom visits and installations
  • To keep records of the work we do for you

We don't sell your details or share them with anyone for marketing. We only share them with suppliers or trades where it's needed to complete your project, for example a stone supplier or plumber we're coordinating for you.

Where it's stored

Quote requests sent through this website are stored by our website host, Netlify, and then by us. This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and a map from Google Maps, which means those services can see your IP address when you visit.

Accessing or correcting your details

You can ask to see, update or delete the details we hold about you at any time. Call us on 1300 205 498 or visit us at 11/23 Childs Rd, Chipping Norton NSW 2170 and we'll take care of it.
