FAQ

Good questions.

Do you only make the vanity, or the whole bathroom?

We make and install the joinery, stone tops and shower screens, and we are happy to work alongside your builder or bathroom renovator.

Are your vanities water resistant?

Yes. We use moisture-resistant boards and sealed finishes made for wet areas.

Floating or floor-standing vanity: which is better?

Floating (wall-hung) vanities make a bathroom feel bigger and are easy to clean under. Floor-standing vanities give more storage and suit classic and Hamptons bathrooms. We make both to the exact size of your space.
