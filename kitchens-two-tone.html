The look

What makes a two-tone kitchen.

A two-tone kitchen uses one colour for the base cabinets or island and another for the overheads or tall cupboards. It adds depth and personality without going over the top.

The easiest rule is dark below and light above, which keeps the room bright. But timber-look overheads over white bases, or a bold island in navy or green, both work beautifully too.

Key features

  • Different colours for bases, overheads or island
  • Dark and white, timber and white, or colour and white
  • Fingerpull, shaker or flat doors
  • Stone tops that tie both colours together
  • Matching handles across both tones
  • Works with any layout

FAQ

Good questions.

Which colour goes on top in a two-tone kitchen?

Usually the lighter colour goes on top to keep the room bright, with the darker colour on the bases or island. In rooms with lots of natural light you can flip it.

Can I try colour combinations before deciding?

Yes. Our free 3D designer lets you pick separate colours for the base and overhead cupboards from real Polytec and Laminex ranges, and see them together in 3D.
