FAQ

Good questions.

Should I choose polyurethane or board doors?

Polyurethane (2-pac) doors are spray painted in almost any Dulux colour and give a smooth, seamless finish. Decorative boards from Polytec and Laminex are hard-wearing and come in realistic woodgrains, stone looks and solid colours. Many kitchens use both, for example board carcasses with painted doors. We'll help you choose at the measure-up.

What's the difference between stone and laminate benchtops?

Engineered stone from Caesarstone or Quantum Quartz is heavy, hard-wearing and can have thick edges and waterfall ends. Laminate costs less and still looks good, which makes it popular for laundries, rentals and budget-conscious kitchens.

Can I see samples before I decide?

Yes. Our Chipping Norton showroom has stone slabs, board colours and Blum hardware to look at and touch. Visits are by appointment.

Can you match an existing colour?

Usually, yes. Painted polyurethane can be colour-matched, and we can often find a close board match. Bring a sample or a photo to the showroom.
