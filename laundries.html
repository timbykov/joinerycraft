FAQ

Good questions.

Can you fit a laundry into a cupboard?

Yes. If you don't have a separate laundry room, we can build a European-style laundry behind doors in a hallway, kitchen or bathroom, with the washer, dryer, tub and storage hidden away when the doors are closed.

Can you stack the washer and dryer?

Yes. We build a tall unit with a shelf rated for the dryer, cut-outs for ventilation, and cupboards above and beside it, so the stack looks built in rather than squeezed in.

Do you organise the plumbing and electrical?

Yes. We can coordinate the plumber and electrician for new taps, waste and power points, so you are not juggling trades yourself.

Which materials hold up best in a laundry?

We use moisture-resistant boards and stone or laminate benchtops that cope with water and detergent. We'll talk you through the options at the measure-up, or you can see them in the showroom.
