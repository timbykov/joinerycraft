How it works

From first call to final handle.

One team looks after your project from start to finish, so you always know who to call.

  1. 01

    Design online or call us

    Start in our free 3D designer, or tell us what you're after by phone or the quote form. Then we measure up and talk through layout, budget and finishes.

  2. 02

    Your 3D design, perfected

    We turn your ideas into an accurate 3D design to your exact measurements, and tweak it until it's right. Visit the showroom to touch real samples.

  4. 04

    Installed & guaranteed

    Our own installers fit it, clean up and walk you through it. The work is backed by our lifetime workmanship guarantee.

FAQ

Good questions.

How does the free 3D designer work?

Open the 3D designer on our homepage and choose a kitchen or wardrobe. Pick a layout, set your sizes, then choose colours, benchtop and handles from our real supplier ranges. You can turn it around in 3D and go full screen on your phone. It's free with no sign-up. When you're happy, tap Get a quote for this design and it comes to us with your quote request.

Is my online design the final design?

It's a starting point. It shows us what you have in mind, then we measure your space and draw up an accurate design and quote. Nothing is made until you've approved the final design.

Are the quote and 3D design really free?

Yes. We measure up, design your space in 3D and give you a quote with no obligation. You only commit once you're happy with the design and the price.

Do you make everything yourselves?

Yes. Design, manufacturing and installation are all done by our own team, and your joinery is built in our Chipping Norton workshop. That's how we keep control of quality and timing.

Can you organise plumbing, electrical and appliances?

Yes. For kitchens and laundries we can coordinate plumbing, electrical and appliances so you're not juggling trades yourself. Let us know what you need when you ask for a quote.

How long does a kitchen or wardrobe take?

It depends on the size of the job and the materials you choose, and we'll give you a timeline with your quote. Because the joinery is made in our workshop first, the time we spend in your home is kept short.

What materials and hardware do you use?

We work with Caesarstone and Quantum Quartz stone, Polytec and Laminex boards, Dulux finishes and Blum hardware, with more than 100 styles, colours and finishes to choose from. You can see samples in the showroom.

Can I visit the showroom?

Yes, at 11/23 Childs Rd, Chipping Norton. Visits are by appointment, so call 1300 205 498 or book through the quote form and we'll find a time.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. Our installations are backed by our lifetime workmanship guarantee, and the hardware and materials we use carry their manufacturers' warranties.

Do you do commercial joinery?

Yes. We build fit-outs for barbers, retail and hospitality, and plan installs around your trading hours.

What areas of Sydney do you service?

We are based in Chipping Norton in south-west Sydney and work right across Greater Sydney, including Liverpool, Bankstown, Fairfield, Campbelltown, Parramatta, the Hills District, the Inner West, St George, the Sutherland Shire and the Eastern Suburbs. Not sure if we cover you? Call 1300 205 498.

How much does a custom kitchen or wardrobe cost?

It depends on the size, layout, door finish, benchtop and hardware you choose. Board doors and laminate tops cost less than polyurethane doors and stone. We give you a detailed quote after a free measure, and you can try different options in our free 3D designer first.
