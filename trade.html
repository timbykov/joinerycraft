FAQ

Questions from builders and designers.

Do you work from architect and designer plans?

Yes. Send us the architectural plans, interior designer drawings or joinery schedule and we will price from them. Before anything is cut we draw the joinery up for your sign-off, so finishes, sizes and hardware match what was specified.

Can you work to our construction program?

Yes. We book the site measure once walls are up and plastered, give you a lead time with the quote, and schedule the install around your program and the other trades.

Do you take on multi-unit and granny flat jobs?

Yes. We build kitchens, wardrobes, vanities and laundries for single homes, duplexes, granny flats and small multi-unit developments, as well as shop and office fit-outs.

Who will we deal with on the job?

One point of contact from quote to handover. They organise the measure, drawings, manufacture and install, and keep you updated so you are not chasing anyone.

What do you need from us to quote?

Plans or sketches with sizes, the joinery schedule or finishes list if you have one, the site address and your rough timing. Photos of the site help too.
